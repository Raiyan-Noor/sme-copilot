Always
  • The chat is the centre of the screen: one 720px column, centred on the full window.
  • The day starts with the morning brief as the first message. Approvals and alerts arrive inside it.
  • Stats live in a quiet 280px rail pinned to the right edge.
  • Bigger views open as one 560px panel from the right. The chat moves left until it closes.
  • A figure's trace opens in the Sheet, the only overlay.
Never
  • Dashboard pages, or a navigation sidebar.
  • The chat as a side panel, drawer or floating widget.
  • Charts, tables or cards in the rail.
  • Two panels at once, or a panel that covers the chat.
  • A chat column wider than 720px on large windows.
